# Store listing (English)

**Title:** Ellinika · 5 minutes
**Subtitle / short description:** Learn Greek in 5 minutes a day: phrases, rules, dictionary

**Full description:**
Learn Greek at a comfortable pace: just 5 minutes a day.

• Spaced-repetition flashcards remind you of phrases that are due for review.
• 2000+ everyday phrases (levels A1–B2) with examples and translations (Russian interface).
• Grammar rules with diagrams: tables, formulas, comparisons, timelines and clear explanations.
• Practice after every rule, then a short self-check.
• Exercises: translation both ways, fill-in-the-blank sentences and short texts.
• Two-way dictionary: Greek → Russian and Russian → Greek, accent-insensitive search.
• Cypriot colloquial variants where they differ.
• Works offline. No sign-up, no ads, no tracking.
• Save and load your progress to move to another phone.
• Light and dark themes, several color palettes, large text option.

**Note:** the app interface and explanations are in Russian.
